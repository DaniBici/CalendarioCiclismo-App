# Notas para Stores · 5.2.0

## Español

### App Store

Nueva sección Vídeos: resúmenes, últimos kilómetros y emisiones completas de carretera y ciclocross, por días y carreras. Cada etapa reúne sus vídeos en un panel propio y en sus clasificaciones, y la tarjeta de una etapa terminada ofrece «Vídeos» y la emisión completa. Ciclocross pasa a estar dentro de Hoy, con el selector CX | Ruta. Hoy y Calendario: los filtros Masculino y Femenino incluyen también las carreras mixtas o con pruebas de ambas categorías, como los Campeonatos de Europa. Hoy: en iPad, la única carrera del día ocupa la columna izquierda, alineada con Resultados. Hoy y Vídeos abren más rápido.

## English

### App Store

New Videos section: highlights, final kilometres and full broadcasts of road and cyclocross races, by day and race. Each stage gathers its videos in its own panel and in its results, and a finished stage's card offers “Videos” and the full broadcast. Cyclocross now lives inside Today, with the CX | Road switch. Today and Calendar: the Men and Women filters now also include mixed races and events with both men's and women's races, such as the European Championships. Today: on iPad, the day's only race sits in the left column, aligned with Results. Today and Videos open faster.

Each stage gathers its videos in its own panel and in its results, and a finished stage's card offers “Videos” and the full broadcast. Cyclocross now lives inside Today, with the CX | Road switch. The Men and Women filters now also include mixed races, such as the European Championships. On tablets, the day's only race sits in the left column.
