What they include

  • TodayEach day's races with start and finish times, TV coverage and access to the results once they finish.
  • NotificationsAlerts for the races, stages and categories you choose to follow.
  • WidgetToday's races on the home screen and, on iPhone, on the lock screen too.
  • OfflineAutomatic download to check the calendar without a connection.
  • ResultsFull classifications for the races on the UCI road calendar.
  • CyclocrossUCI calendar with schedules by category, startlists, results, overall standings and videos.
  • TransfersSignings, rumours and contract status of the teams.

Free and ad-free

Every feature is free and the apps show no advertising. Anyone who wishes to help cover the project's costs can do so voluntarily: Support Calendario Ciclismo.

Thanks to the nearly 200 people who took part in the Android beta. Their help was essential in publishing the app.
